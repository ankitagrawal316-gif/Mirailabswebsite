Questions

What enterprise teams ask us first.

Where does our data go, and is it used to train models?

Your data stays within your environment and your contractual boundary. It is not used to train foundation models, and it is not pooled across customers. Access is governed by the same identity and permission rules as the rest of your stack, and every retrieval and action is logged.

Can we run this in our own cloud?

Yes. The platform can be deployed in your cloud tenancy or ours. Which model you use is a configuration choice rather than an architectural one, so you can run frontier models, models hosted in your own region, or private models, and change that decision later without rebuilding the workflows on top.

How long until the first workflow is actually in production?

Weeks rather than quarters, because the first deployment configures agent patterns, integrations, approval mechanisms and governance controls that already exist rather than building them. The honest variable is your side: how accessible the source systems are, and how quickly the people who own the process can give us their exceptions and rules.

What do you need from us to start?

One workflow that matters, access to the systems it touches, and time from the people who actually run the process. You do not need a data lake, a completed migration, or an in-house AI team. If your data is messy, that is normal and it is part of the work rather than a prerequisite for starting it.

How is this different from the copilots we already have?

A copilot makes one person faster at their task. It does not read your ERP, act inside your CRM, respect your approval chain, or carry an audit trail. Mirai operates at the level of the process rather than the individual, which is why the measurable change shows up in a business number rather than in time saved per person.

What does an engagement cost?

Pricing has two parts: a platform subscription, and the deployment work for each use case. The second falls sharply after the first workflow, because the integrations, context and controls are already in place. We scope the first engagement against the business number you are trying to move, so you can judge it on return rather than on rate.

Who runs it after go-live?

We stay accountable after go-live rather than handing over and leaving. Over time your own teams can take on as much of the operation as you want, and because the workflows, rules and context live in the platform rather than in our heads, taking it further in-house does not mean rebuilding it.

What if AI is not the right answer for our problem?

Then we will tell you. Some problems are better solved by fixing a process, a rule or an integration, and saying so early costs you less than discovering it three months into a deployment.

What should your business operate differently because of AI?

Start with one problem where better context, faster decisions or fewer handoffs would move a number you already report. We will tell you honestly whether AI is the right answer for it.
