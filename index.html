FAQ

Questions we hear first.

What makes Mirai different from consultancies and AI tool vendors?

Consultancies leave you a strategy. Tool vendors leave you software to fit yourself. Our engineers and domain experts work inside your team and put the first workflow into production in about 8 weeks. Then we keep running it against one metric you choose. We are model-neutral, and if AI is not the right answer, we tell you.

How do you keep our data secure?

Your data stays in your own cloud tenancy or a dedicated Mirai environment, never in a shared pool. Access is role-based, every action is logged, and your people approve the steps that matter. You choose the model, including models that run inside your cloud.

How do you measure success?

In week one we agree one business metric with you, such as days to close or cost per ticket, and measure its baseline together. After go-live we report against that baseline every month. If the metric is not moving, we say so early and change course.

How does an engagement start?

We start with one workflow on a fixed scope. From you we need one owner for the metric, access to the systems the workflow touches, and a few hours a week from the people who do the work today.

What happens after the first use case goes live?

We keep running it and report against the baseline every month. Then we move to a monthly pod that adds the next use case on the same intelligence layer, so each one goes live faster than the last.
